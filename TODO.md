- [Before public publication](docs-web/content/roadmap.md#before-public-publication): fresh public history, repository/reporting setup, real installation URLs and public-candidate CI/docs checks.

Edit the canonical roadmap in `docs-web/content/roadmap.md` and regenerate the developer website. Do not duplicate the checklist here or import private planning notes. See [contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [releasing](RELEASING.md) for the workflow. Fuzz work remains deferred.
